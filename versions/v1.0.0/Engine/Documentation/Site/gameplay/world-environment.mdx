Terrain, water, foliage and sky are authored in the editor; see [World building](/docs/editor/world-building/) and [Rendering](/docs/rendering/). This page covers what gameplay code does with them at runtime.

## Day and night

A `DayNight` component drives the sun, sky and night lighting from a time of day. <Source symbol="DayNightComponent" />

| Field | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `timeOfDay` | 12 | Hours, 0 to 24 |
| `animate`, `dayLengthSeconds` | off, 120 | Advance the clock; a full day takes this many real seconds |
| `azimuthDegrees` | 0 | Compass direction of the sun's path; any angle from -180 to 360 turns the whole arc around the vertical axis |
| `sunIntensity`, `nightIntensity` | 1.4, 0.02 | Sun light by day and night |
| `skyDayIntensity`, `skyNightIntensity` | 1.0, 0.15 | Sky light by day and night |

NPC schedules read the same clock; see [AI crowds and director](/docs/gameplay/ai-crowds-and-director/).

## Water

Place water with `AWaterActor`; its `MWaterComponent` describes the body. A `WaterZone` component bounds which bodies take part and fills the horizon past them. <Source symbol="WaterZoneComponent" />

`MWaterStatics` answers water questions at any point, from C++ or Visual Scripting: <Source symbol="MWaterStatics" />

| Function | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `IsUnderwater(point)`, `HasWaterAt(point)` | Whether a point is under or over water |
| `GetWaterHeightAt`, `GetWaterDepthAt`, `GetWaterFlowAt` | Surface height including waves, depth and river flow |
| `GetNearestShorePoint`, `GetDistanceToShore` | The shore |
| `SampleWaterAt` then `GetLastWaterHeight`, `GetLastWaterNormal`, `GetLastWaveHeight` and the other `GetLast...` functions | Everything about one point from a single query |

### Buoyancy and swimming

Add a `Buoyancy` component to a dynamic rigid body to make it float: `strength`, `sampleRadius`, `maxDepth`, and `linearDrag` and `angularDrag` while in the water. <Source symbol="BuoyancyComponent" />

Characters switch to swimming on their own when the water reaches their chest; see [Character movement](/docs/gameplay/character-movement/).

## Streaming regions and data layers

In an open world, levels stream by cells around the player. Gameplay can also load parts by hand through `MLevelStatics`: <Source symbol="MLevelStatics" />

| Function | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `LoadStreamingRegion(name)`, `UnloadStreamingRegion(name)`, `IsStreamingRegionLoaded(name)` | Keep a named region loaded, for example ahead of a cutscene or a fast-travel destination |
| `SetDataLayerActive(name, active)`, `IsDataLayerActive(name)` | Switch a data layer, such as a story state of a village, on or off |

`MLevelStatics` also reads and writes scene environment settings by key, such as fog and ambient color: `GetSceneFloat`, `SetSceneFloat`, `GetSceneVector`, `SetSceneVector`, `GetSceneBool` and `SetSceneBool`.
