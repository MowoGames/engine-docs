These editors author what a mesh, texture or physics asset carries beyond its imported data. All of them use the [shared asset editor frame](/docs/editor/asset-editors/), keep their own undo history, and mount the shared preview scene rig where they have a 3D preview.

## Static Mesh editor

Double-click a model without bones (`.fbx`, `.gltf`, `.glb`, `.obj` or a cooked `.mmodel`) to open the Static Mesh editor. A model with bones opens the Skeletal Mesh editor instead; **Open With** offers both.

<Screenshot id="editor-static-mesh-editor" alt="The Static Mesh editor on a crate mesh: toolbar with Save, Reimport, Find in CB, Collision, UV, Sockets, Nanite and LOD Auto, the LODs list on the left, the preview with a box collision wireframe and a socket handle, the view chips plate, the foot bar with triangle and vertex counts, and the Details tab on the right showing the Collision section." />

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar | **Save**, **Reimport**, **Find in CB**; the **Collision** menu (add box, sphere, capsule, convex hull, 10/18/26-DOP or auto convex collision, remove a shape, show collision); **UV** (show UV checker); **Sockets** (add, remove, show); **Nanite** (the meshlet build); the LOD picker |
| Left panel | **LODs** (each level's triangles and screen size, then the sockets) and **Sockets** |
| Preview | View chips for perspective, lighting, wireframe, collision, UVs, sockets and grid; a foot bar with triangles, vertices, sections, collision shapes, sockets and size |
| Right panel | **Details**: Collision, Sockets, Material slots, LODs, Surface cache and Build settings. **Asset**: read-only counts and bounds |

Save writes the mesh's `.mmeshmeta` sidecar (collision, sockets, material slot bindings, the surface-cache setting) and the import block of its `.mdfmeta` (build settings). Build settings such as Nanite take effect on the next reimport.

### Collision

Choose the **Complexity** (Simple And Complex, Use Simple As Complex, Use Complex As Simple), then generate shapes fitted to the mesh: **Box**, **Sphere**, **Capsule**, **Hull**, the K-DOPs, or **Auto Convex**, which decomposes the mesh into convex hulls and replaces the existing shapes. **Max Hulls**, **Hull Verts** and **Concavity** set the decomposition budget; it is stored on the mesh, so the cook uses the same budget for a mesh with no authored collision. Select a shape to edit its centre and size, or remove it.

### Sockets and material slots

A **socket** is a named transform on the mesh, such as a muzzle or a door handle. Add one with **Add Socket**, name it and set its position and scale; its handle draws in the preview. Gameplay reads sockets with <Source symbol="AActor::GetSocketTransform" /> and attaches to them by name.

Each **material slot** picks a `.mat` asset. The binding is stored by slot name, so renaming the material or the mesh keeps it.

The **Surface cache** section sets how the mesh is represented in Global Illumination's surface cache: Auto, Off or Custom. See [Rendering](/docs/rendering/).

## Skeletal Mesh editor

A model with bones opens in the Skeletal Mesh editor.

<Screenshot id="editor-skeletal-mesh-editor" alt="The Skeletal Mesh editor on a character: the Skeleton tree on the left with a search box and one bone selected, the viewport with bones and socket handles drawn over the mesh and the control plate (preview clip, play, Turntable, Weights, LOD), the Weights heat ramp on the selected bone, the LODs dock tab below, and the Details rail on the right." />

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar | **Save**, **Reimport**, **LODs**, **Make static mesh** (opens the model in the Static Mesh editor, for collision and build settings), **Bones**, **Sockets**, **Find in CB** |
| Skeleton tree | The bone hierarchy with a search box; bones with sockets show a count, virtual bones are tagged. **+** adds a socket on the selected bone |
| Viewport | Preview clip (T-pose or any clip in the model), play/pause, **Turntable**, **Weights** (the selected bone's skin weights as a heat ramp) and an LOD pin |
| Dock | **LODs** (triangles, vertices, sections per level) and **Log** (the model's load log) |
| Details | The selected bone (read-only), the LOD table with screen coverage, material slots, Sockets, Virtual bones, Physics asset, Cloth paint and Morph targets |

- **Sockets** ride a bone with an offset. At runtime a socket name resolves before a bone of the same name, so existing attachments by bone name keep working.
- **Virtual bones** are added to the imported skeleton: parented to a source bone and driven onto a target bone every frame, for IK goals and aim points without re-rigging. They work in animation, the animation graph, control rig and retargeting.
- **Physics asset** picks the `.physasset` the ragdoll uses, stored on the mesh.
- **Cloth paint** paints the max-distance mask a Cloth component applies per vertex, with **Fill Free**, **Fill Fixed** and **Remove Mask**. Each stroke is one undo entry.
- **Morph targets** each get a preview slider; preview weights are not saved.

Save writes sockets, virtual bones, cloth masks and LOD settings to the model's sidecar. Bone transforms cannot be edited here; retargeting is the [IK Retargeter](/docs/editor/animation-editors/) and notifies are in the Animation Clip editor.

## Texture editor

Images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.tga`, `.bmp`, `.dds`, `.hdr`, `.exr`) and cooked textures open in the Texture editor. Large source images decode in the background; the editor shows **Decoding image** until they arrive.

<Screenshot id="editor-texture-editor" alt="The Texture editor on a normal map: Info panel on the left with Imported, Displayed, Format, Resource size, On disk and Mips rows, the canvas in the centre with the R/G/B/A channel toggles, checker button and Mip slider above it, and the Details panel on the right with Compression, Level of detail and AI upscale sections." />

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar | **Save**, **Reimport**, **Find in CB**, **AI upscale**, **Compress now**, **Fit**, **1:1** |
| Canvas | Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. R, G, B and A channel toggles, a transparency checker and a mip slider; a line under the canvas reads the texel under the cursor |
| Info | Measurements: imported and displayed size, format, GPU resource size, size on disk, mip count, streaming |
| Details | Compression, Level of detail and AI upscale settings |

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Settings (compression) | Default (auto), Uncompressed, BC1, BC3, BC4, BC5 (normal map), BC7, BC6H (HDR) |
| sRGB | The colour space the sampler decodes through |
| Maximum size | **Default** (unauthored), **No limit**, or a size from 256 to 8192 |
| BC7 rate-distortion | Re-encodes BC7 blocks so packages compress smaller; **Strength** sets how far |
| LOD group | Color, Normal Map, Linear / Mask or HDR. Normal Map, Linear and HDR always sample linear |
| Mip generation | Off, Box or Kaiser filter |
| Alpha coverage | An alpha-test cutoff every mip preserves, so foliage does not thin out with distance |
| Virtual texture | Also cook page tiles for virtual texture streaming |

**Save** writes the settings into the texture's `.mdfmeta` and reloads the texture in place, so every material using it updates at once. The editor decodes through the same texture cook a package uses, so what you see includes the compression and size limits a shipped build has.

**Compress now** encodes BC7 twice on a background thread, with rate-distortion off and at the texture's strength, and shows quality, size on disk and encode time for both. A **Compare** row then switches the canvas between **Source**, **Split** (drag the handle), **Compressed** and **Error x8**.

**AI upscale** queues a 2× or 4× upscale with a fast or a quality model, up to 8192 on the longest edge, and reimports the texture when it finishes. It is disabled, with the reason in its tooltip, when the upscaler is not available.

## Physics Asset editor

A `.physasset` names the collision bodies of a skeletal mesh and the constraints that wire them into a ragdoll; the runtime builds its ragdoll from exactly this file.

<Screenshot id="editor-physics-asset-editor" alt="The Physics Asset editor on a character ragdoll: the Skeleton & bodies panel with a body list and the bone tree, capsule body gizmos over the mesh in the viewport with one body accented and a constraint cone shown, the simulation bar under the viewport, and the selected body's shape, mass and collision rows in Details." />

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar | **Save**, **Simulate**, **Add body** (a capsule on the selected bone), **Add constraint** (to the parent bone's body), **Regenerate bodies**, **Show bodies**, **Find in CB** |
| Left panel | The bodies and constraints with a search box, then the skeleton tree with a marker on bones that have a body. **Delete** removes the selection |
| Viewport | The skinned mesh with capsule, box and sphere gizmos; the selected constraint draws its twist axis and swing cone. A body whose bone is missing draws in the warning colour |
| Simulation bar | Simulate/Stop, pause, step, reset, gravity and an impulse test |
| Details | The selected body (bone, shape, size, mass, density, collision and what it collides with) or constraint (parent, child, type, swing and twist or hinge limits) |

**Simulate** drops the ragdoll into a live simulation in the preview; press it again to stop. **Regenerate bodies** replaces all bodies and constraints with an automatically generated set.

## Fracture editor

A `.fracture` is a bake: the pieces a volume was carved into, and the settings that carved them.

| Toolbar | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Save | Writes the `.fracture` |
| Bake fracture | Re-carves the pieces from the settings, in the background, as one undo entry |
| Reset | Puts every piece back at rest |
| Explode | Pushes the pieces apart so each shows |
| Levels | Switches the left panel between **Fracture** and **Outliner** |
| Simulate | Lets every piece the anchors do not hold fall |

The **Fracture** tab picks the method; Details holds its settings, a random seed and the piece count the next bake will produce:

| Method | Cuts |
| --- | --- |
| Uniform | Voronoi cells seeded evenly |
| Clustered | Voronoi cells gathered in clusters: pockets of small pieces among large ones |
| Radial | Rings of cells around an impact point |
| Planar | Random planes through the volume |
| Slice | Evenly spaced, optionally tilted cuts along each axis |
| Brick | Courses of bricks in a chosen bond |

Changing a setting does not change the pieces until you bake. A plate over the preview has **Colour by piece**, an explode slider and **Anchors**. The **Outliner** tab lists the pieces; Details shows the selected piece and the **Connectivity** settings. A content hash of the pieces (the chunk set) lets multiplayer peers check they hold the same bake. See [Gameplay](/docs/gameplay/) for destruction at runtime.
