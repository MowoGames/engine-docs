Both systems come with the **Combat, Interaction & RPG** plugin (off by default; enable it in the Plugins window). <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Combat.mplugin" />

## Zones

A zone is a named region of the level made of one or more box or sphere volumes. Place an `AZoneActor`, or add a `Zone` component to any entity. Volumes are axis-aligned. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/ZoneComponent.h" />

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `zoneId` | Volumes with the same id form one zone |
| `displayName`, `zoneTypeTag` | A name for UI and a free-form type such as `safe`, `hostile` or `interior` |
| `shape`, `halfExtents`, `radius` | The volume |
| `priority` | Where volumes overlap, the highest wins, then the smaller |
| `noCombat` | Weapons and melee refuse to fire or attack inside |
| `noBuild` | Building placement is refused inside |
| `ambienceId` | Selects the music score's ambience bed; see [Music and mixing](/docs/gameplay/music-and-mixing/) |
| `statusEffectId` | A `.statuseffect` applied while inside and removed on leaving |

Characters join zones automatically. When an entity's zone changes, the zone system fires `OnZoneLeft` and `OnZoneEntered`, and the `ZoneLeft` and `ZoneEntered` events on the entity's Visual Scripting graph. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/ZoneSystem.h" />

Touching zones form a room graph: `GetZoneNeighbors(zoneId)` lists neighbors and `FindZonePath(from, to)` returns the chain of zones between two, for AI and level logic.

`MZoneStatics` offers `GetPlayerZone`, `IsInZone`, `CountEntitiesInZone`, `IsPlayerCombatBlocked` and `GetZoneTypeTag`. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/MZoneStatics.h" />

## Utility grids

Utility grids are power or water networks of placed nodes, for base-building and factory games. Place an `AUtilityNodeActor`, or add a `UtilityNode` component. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/UtilityNodeComponent.h" />

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `gridType`, `customGridTag` | `Power`, `Water` or `Custom`; different grids never mix |
| `role` | `Producer` (supplies `capacity`), `Consumer` (draws `demand`) or `Conduit` (a link) |
| `connectionRadius` | Two nodes connect when their distance is within the larger radius |
| `fuelItemId`, `fuelBurnTime` | An item a producer burns from its inventory, one unit per burn time; out of fuel means no output |

Connected nodes form networks. In each network, supply is shared among consumers smallest demand first, and a consumer is powered only when its whole demand fits. Networks rebuild when nodes move, appear or disappear. The system fires `OnNetworkPowered`, `OnNetworkUnpowered` and `OnNodePowerChanged`, with matching Visual Scripting events on the nodes. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/UtilityGridSystem.h" />

A crafting station with `requiresPower` only crafts while a powered node is nearby. See [Interaction and inventory](/docs/gameplay/interaction-and-inventory/).

`MUtilityStatics` offers `IsNodePowered`, `GetNetworkSupply`, `GetNetworkDemand` and `GetNetworkCount`. <Source path="Engine/Plugins/Combat/Public/CombatPlugin/MUtilityStatics.h" />
