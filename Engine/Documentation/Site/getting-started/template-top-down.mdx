The Top Down template is a small action game you can open and play. A Quaternius hero runs a walled 44 by 44 metre yard under a fixed camera. Five supply crates glow gold and four zombies hunt whatever they hear. Everything runs on engine classes, Visual Scripting and the shared content packs, so Play works the moment the project opens.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W** **A** **S** **D**, left stick | Move; screen up is north |
| **Space**, **A** button | Jump |
| **E**, **X** button | Interact |

## How it plays

Walking over a crate collects it and advances the `Clear the yard` quest, which the HUD shows at the top left. Walking is noisy. A zombie hears your footsteps within its hearing radius, drifts toward the last sound, searches around it and runs at you once its alertness is high. A zombie that touches you sends you back to the start; the crates you took stay taken.

## How it is built

| Asset | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Content/Scenes/Main.mmap` | The yard: Level Prototyping tiles inside grid walls, Zombie Kit cover, a player start, a nav bounds volume the yard bakes on load, five crate pickups and four zombies |
| `BP_TopDownHero` | The player. A Visual Script class on `ATopDownCharacter` whose defaults name the hero mesh, the locomotion animation graph, the footstep folder and the camera distance and pitch. The engine character builds and animates the body |
| `BP_TopDownGameMode` | Sets the default pawn and starts the quest |
| `BP_TopDownPickup` | A crate: its `OnTriggerEnter` graph casts the overlapping actor to the hero, advances the objective, plays a chime and destroys the crate |
| `BP_TopDownZombie` | A zombie's trigger: it casts the overlapping actor to the hero and moves the hero back to the start |
| `Zombie.resident` | The hearing profile: how far a zombie hears, how fast its alertness builds and decays and how fast it moves in each stage |
| `Zombie.animgraph` | Idle, Walk and Run over `Speed`. The engine feeds `Speed` from the nav agent's velocity |

## Make it yours

Move or add crates and zombies in the viewport. A crate is any actor of class `BP_TopDownPickup` with a Trigger; a zombie is a mesh with an Animator, a NavAgent, a Resident and a Trigger. Change the quest's `count` and place that many crates, or open `Zombie.resident` to make zombies deaf or faster. To drive a different character, set `meshAsset` on `BP_TopDownHero` to another `.glb` whose clips match `ABP_Locomotion.animgraph`.

See [Templates](/docs/getting-started/templates/) for how a template becomes your project.
