The Vehicle template is a time trial. A Kenney race car on the engine's vehicle component, with real wheel suspension, laps a Racing Kit circuit of straights and corners. Four checkpoint gates run a three lap timer with a best lap, and the HUD shows speed and the camera in use. It plays on the Vehicle Kit plugin, which `Config/Plugins.ini` enables, so no module needs building.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W** / **S**, triggers | Throttle and brake; reverse when stopped |
| **A** / **D**, left stick | Steer |
| **Space**, **A** button | Handbrake |
| **R**, **Y** button | Respawn at the last checkpoint you passed |
| **C**, **X** button | Cycle the camera: chase, far, bumper |

The car also puts itself back on the road after three seconds on its roof, or if it falls off the world.

## How it is built

| Asset | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Content/Scenes/Main.mmap` | The circuit on a grass field with trees, a start gate with a banner and flags, a grandstand, a pit office, four `ARaceCheckpointActor` gates named `Gate_0` (start and finish) to `Gate_3`, the race car and the HUD |
| `BP_RaceCar` | A Visual Script class on `AVehiclePawn`. Its defaults name the body and wheel meshes and the chassis and wheel dimensions. When play begins the pawn builds the rigid body, the collider, the vehicle component and four wheel visuals |
| `BP_RaceGameMode` | A Visual Script class on `ATimeTrialGameMode` with the lap count. It reads the gates in order, runs the lap timer and hands the placed car to the player |
| `Content/Input/` | The Drive, Handbrake, Reset and CameraMode actions and the default mapping context, for keyboard and gamepad |
| `Content/UI/HUD.ui` | Lap, race time, lap time, best lap, next gate, message, speed and camera labels, bound to the `timetrial.*` and `vehicle.*` HUD sources |

## Make it yours

Add Racing Kit pieces from `Content/Racing/Track` and move the gates. A gate is an `ARaceCheckpointActor` whose name ends in its position in the lap. Swap `bodyMesh` on `BP_RaceCar` for `Car_Sedan.glb` or `Car_SUV.glb` and adjust `bodyHalfWidth`, `trackHalfWidth` and `bodyOffsetY` to match, or tune `topSpeed`, `steerRate` and the camera values.

See [Templates](/docs/getting-started/templates/) for how a template becomes your project.
