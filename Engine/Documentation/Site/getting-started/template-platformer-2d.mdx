The 2D Platformer template is a side-scroller on Kenney Platformer Pack Redux art. Twelve coins sit on the ground and on six floating platforms. Three slimes patrol the ground: land on one from above to stomp it and bounce, but touch one from the side and the hero goes back to the start. A goal flag at the right end of the level shows the totals. The coin and stomp counters and the level-complete message are on the HUD, which reads the `platformer.*` HUD sources. The `APlatformerPickupActor`, `APlatformerPatrolActor` and `APlatformerGoalActor` classes come from the Platformer Kit and can be placed from the **Gameplay** category.

Originally the template was a pixel-art side-scroller. A 64 by 24 tile level has two ground runs with a pit and a water pool, four floating ledges and spikes. A sprite hero runs and jumps through it past six coins, with parallax hills and sky behind, all seen through a pixel-perfect orthographic camera.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **A** / **D**, **Left** / **Right** | Run |
| **Space** | Jump; press again in the air for the double jump; hold for a higher jump |

Rebind them in `Content/Input/IMC_Default.inputcontext`.

## How it is built

Movement comes from the **Platformer Kit** plugin and the 2D parts from [2D Sprites and Tilemaps](/docs/gameplay/2d-sprites-and-tilemaps/); `Config/Plugins.ini` enables both.

| Entity or asset | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Level` | A Tilemap entity for `Level1.tilemap`. The `Ground` layer collides because the tileset flags its tiles Solid, and the `Ground` terrain auto-tiles it. The `Hazards` layer holds spikes and animated water |
| `Camera` | An orthographic, pixel-perfect Camera with Camera Follow 2D: dead zone, look-ahead and bounds |
| `Hero` | A Flipbook with a Sprite Follower. It mirrors the character and plays idle, run, jump or fall from how it moves |
| `SkyFar`, `Hills1` to `Hills3` | Sprites with Parallax; the hills wrap so they never run out |
| `Coin1` to `Coin6` | Flipbooks |
| `BP_Platformer2DPlayer` | A Platformer Kit character with its capsule hidden (`bodyVisible` off) and its own side camera off (`spawnCameraRig` off) |

## Make it yours

- Select `Level`, switch the viewport to the **Tile Palette** mode and paint. The ground terrain picks the edge tiles.
- Replace `hero.png`, `tiles.png` and the other sheets with your own of the same layout, or slice a new sheet with **Slice Into Sprites** in the Content Browser. The generated art is a placeholder.
- Add a flipbook state for a new animation in the `Hero` entity's Animation States list and name it in the Sprite Follower.
- Tune run speed, jumps and coyote time in the native defaults of `BP_Platformer2DPlayer`.
